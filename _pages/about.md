Welcome to my homepage! (Last edited in Sep. 2025)

I am Yanzhe Wang (王彦喆), currently a Master student at Princeton University for a one-year MFin program. I had my undergraduate in [School of Mathematical Sciences](https://www.math.pku.edu.cn), [Peking University](https://pku.edu.cn), major in Mathematics and minor in Economics. 

I have a wide range of interests in Machine Learning and Quantitative Finance. Previously, I have interned as a quant trader at [Jane Street](https://www.janestreet.com) in Hong Kong for 2024 and 2025 summer, and as a quant researcher at Tongdeng Capital in 2024 winter. I will join Jane Street as a full time trader in 2026 summer after the graduation of my Master degree.

I have conducted several research programs. Last year, I have worked on Discrete Diffusion Models under the guidance of Prof. Mengdi Wang from Princeton University and Prof. Minshuo Chen from Northwestern University. I have also worked on Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Trading Platform under the guidance of Prof. Yangbo He, and on High-Frequency Pricing Models under the guidance of Prof. Ruixun Zhang, both from Peking University.
